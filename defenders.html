Defenders

Last Updated 10/7/2026

Defenders is an upcoming title scheduled to finish development in December of 2026. It's being developed with the Game Creation Society at CMU.

At this point, the game is still in early development. However, as development progresses, more updates will hopefully be shared on this page.

The main concept of the game centers around a mashup of shooter and tower-defense gameplay. The game loop is defeating enemies, upgrading yourself, and through repeated upgrades, transforming yourself into a mighty auto-attacking tower!
